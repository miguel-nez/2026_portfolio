About me

I'm a product designer with a background in cultural studies, linguistics and architecture. It sounds like three different paths, but for me it's felt like one question asked three ways: how do people make sense of reality?

I work best in a team, close to the people who build the product. I like to understand the technical side early, ask questions and keep the conversation open, even when people disagree.

After a few years in an agency, I'm looking for an in-house team where I can stay with a product and keep improving it. I code a little, enough to build this site with some help from AI, and I'd like to learn more. Outside work I'm interested in film, music, urbanism and conflict mapping.
